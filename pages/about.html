My Story

I am Isaiah U. Imaga, professionally known as IU Imaga. My journey spans healthcare, public health, creative writing, leadership, entrepreneurship, and community development.

I believe meaningful impact is created when knowledge, service, and enterprise work together.

Areas of Focus

The Scientist

Medical Laboratory Scientist and Public Health Professional focused on diagnostics, epidemiology, health systems strengthening, and healthcare innovation.

The Writer

Through poetry, stories, essays, and reflections, I explore identity, purpose, faith, leadership, and the human experience.

The Builder

Passionate about building ventures, institutions, brands, and systems that create long-term value and sustainable impact.

The Community Advocate

Dedicated to community development, healthcare improvement, public health leadership, and social impact.

Professional Journey

As a Medical Laboratory Scientist with a Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science (BMLS) and a Master of Public Health (MPH) in Epidemiology, I have developed experience in laboratory medicine, molecular diagnostics, disease surveillance, public health programmes, and healthcare systems strengthening.

My professional interests include diagnostics, epidemiology, public health leadership, healthcare innovation, policy development, and community health improvement.

Building Beyond Healthcare

Beyond healthcare and writing, I am actively building ventures and initiatives designed to create meaningful impact.

This growing ecosystem includes AccurateBio Diagnostics, 73 Collections, Oil Republic, A Writer's Lab, Spark a Smile Initiative, and future ventures under the broader Imaga vision.
