ABOUT

Isaiah U. Imaga

Medical Laboratory Scientist • MPH Epidemiology • National Health Fellow

Bridging laboratory diagnostics, public health systems, and community healthcare delivery in Nigeria.

The problem I solve

In primary health facilities, I've repeatedly seen the same challenge: high-quality laboratory data that fails to translate into timely action. Viral load results and Early Infant Diagnosis (EID) often sit unused. Supply chains stall. Patients are lost to follow-up.

This is exactly the gap I work to close.

What I bring

The Scientist

Medical Laboratory Scientist with over four years of hands-on experience in molecular diagnostics, viral load testing, and Early Infant Diagnosis (EID). Skilled in laboratory quality management aligned with ISO 15189:2022 and Good Clinical Laboratory Practice (GCLP).

The Public Health Leader

MPH in Epidemiology focused on integrating laboratory data with program decision-making, public health supply chain management, and health commodity systems that respond to real community needs.

The Builder

Founder of Spark a Smile Initiative, leading medical outreach, education, and community support programs for underserved populations — turning policy and data into tangible impact on the ground.

The Writer

Through my blog "A Writer's Lab," I explore themes of connection, reflection, and the human stories behind the data. I believe empathy and systems thinking must go hand in hand in public health.

Professional journey

I hold a Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science (BMLS) and a Master of Public Health (MPH) in Epidemiology, and I am a National Health Fellow. My work focuses on strengthening the critical links between robust lab systems and effective public health response — from diagnostics to last-mile delivery.

Building beyond healthcare

Outside the laboratory and the page, I'm building ventures and initiatives designed to create sustainable impact across sectors:

  • AccurateBio Diagnostics — healthcare diagnostics
  • 73 Collections — creative and lifestyle
  • Oil Republic — agribusiness sector
  • A Writer's Lab — craft and mentorship
  • Spark a Smile Initiative — community outreach (founder-led)

More ventures are on the way — all under the broader Imaga vision of meaningful, lasting change.
