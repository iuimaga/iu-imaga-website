About IU Imaga

Scientist. Writer. Builder. Community Advocate.

My Story

I am Isaiah U. Imaga, professionally known as IU Imaga. My journey spans healthcare, public health, creative writing, leadership, entrepreneurship, and community development. I believe that meaningful impact is created when knowledge, service, and enterprise work together.

The Scientist

As a Medical Laboratory Scientist with a Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science (BMLS) and a Master of Public Health (MPH) in Epidemiology, I have developed experience in laboratory medicine, molecular diagnostics, public health, disease surveillance, and healthcare systems strengthening.

My professional interests include diagnostics, epidemiology, public health leadership, healthcare innovation, and community health improvement.

The Writer

Under the pen name IU Imaga, I write poetry, stories, reflections, and essays. Through A Writer's Lab, I explore themes of identity, faith, love, purpose, leadership, and the human experience.

The Builder

Beyond healthcare and writing, I am passionate about building institutions, brands, and ventures that create sustainable value.

My growing ecosystem includes AccurateBio Diagnostics, 73 Collections, Oil Republic, and other initiatives focused on innovation, service, and long-term impact.

Community Impact

Community service remains a central part of my journey. Through public health advocacy, medical outreaches, leadership initiatives, and the National Health Fellowship, I work with others to improve lives and strengthen communities.

Personal Philosophy

“I believe meaningful change happens when knowledge, service, and enterprise work together.”
