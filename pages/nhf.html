National Health Fellowship

Advancing public health, leadership, health systems strengthening, and community impact.

Overview

The National Health Fellowship provided an opportunity to contribute to public health systems strengthening, leadership development, stakeholder engagement, healthcare advocacy, and community impact initiatives.

Through training, field activities, stakeholder engagements, and project implementation, the Fellowship strengthened my capacity to contribute meaningfully to health sector development and community wellbeing.

Capstone Project

Community-Based Advocacy and Sensitization Campaign for Increased Utilization of Primary Healthcare Services.

The project focused on improving awareness and encouraging greater utilization of Primary Healthcare Centres through community engagement, advocacy, and health education activities.

Inauguration Ceremony

The Fellowship journey began with inauguration activities that brought together fellows committed to improving health outcomes and strengthening healthcare systems.

Courtesy Visit to the Honourable Commissioner for Health, Abia State

Fellowship activities included strategic engagements with key stakeholders in the health sector to strengthen partnerships and collaboration.

Courtesy Visit to the Executive Secretary, Abia State Primary Health Care Development Agency

Engagement with the Agency provided valuable opportunities to discuss primary healthcare strengthening and community health interventions.

Community Advocacy and Sensitization Campaign

Advocacy materials were developed and deployed to support public awareness, promote healthcare utilization, and encourage community participation in health programmes.

Field Activities

Field visits and community engagements provided practical experience in public health advocacy, stakeholder engagement, and health systems strengthening.
