PUBLIC HEALTH LEADERSHIP

National Health Fellowship

Advancing health systems strengthening, leadership, advocacy, stakeholder engagement and community impact.

Overview

The National Health Fellowship provided an opportunity to contribute to healthcare development through leadership training, stakeholder engagement, policy discussions, advocacy campaigns, and community-based public health interventions.

The Fellowship strengthened my capacity in leadership, programme implementation, health systems strengthening, community engagement, and evidence-based public health practice.

Capstone Project

Community-Based Advocacy and Sensitization Campaign for Increased Utilization of Primary Healthcare Services

The project focused on increasing awareness and encouraging greater utilization of Primary Healthcare Centres through stakeholder engagement, advocacy, sensitization activities, and community outreach.

Inauguration Ceremony

The Fellowship journey commenced with an inauguration ceremony bringing together fellows committed to strengthening healthcare systems and improving health outcomes across Nigeria.

Stakeholder Engagement

Strategic engagements with government institutions and health sector leaders formed an important aspect of the Fellowship experience.

Advocacy Campaign Materials

Information, education, and communication materials were developed to support community sensitization and encourage increased utilization of primary healthcare services.

Field Activities

Community engagement activities provided practical experience in stakeholder mobilization, advocacy, health education, programme implementation, and public health leadership.

Key Lesson

Effective healthcare systems are built through leadership, collaboration, community participation, and sustained advocacy.
